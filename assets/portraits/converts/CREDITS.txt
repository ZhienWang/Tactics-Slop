Converted Legionnaire portraits (assets/portraits/converts/convert_NN.png).

Cropped and scaled to 200x100 from 17th-century paintings (tronies and head
studies of ordinary people) on Wikimedia Commons. All are public domain: the
painters died centuries ago and the files are marked Public domain on Commons.
