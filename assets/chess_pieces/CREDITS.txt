Chess piece artwork (king/queen/rook/bishop/knight/pawn, light and dark)
originally created by Wikimedia Commons user Cburnett, from the
"SVG chess pieces" set:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces

Rasterized from the original SVGs (Chess_*45.svg) to 256x256 PNGs with a
transparent background for use as this game's on-map unit art.
